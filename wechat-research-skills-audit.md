# WeChat Research Skills Audit

Verification date: 2026-11-25. Scope: public articles and reliable mirrors about research, literature, paper-writing, and mathematical-modeling Skills. Article titles and source labels are discovery evidence, not authority over official competition rules or primary repositories.

## Verification grades

- **A**: original WeChat URL or an inspectable reproduction with explicit original-account metadata, plus a matching primary repository or Skill body.
- **B**: inspectable publisher or aggregator reproduction with account, date, and substantive article body, but the original WeChat page was not fully inspected.
- **C**: title, account, and date were found in an author list or search record; the article body was not used to change the bundle.

## Mechanism-level synthesis

The public articles repeatedly recommend many differently named Skills, but most names collapse into existing decision surfaces:

1. **Search and retrieval**: query translation, source ladder, multi-platform merge, de-duplication, identity cross-check, full-text access honesty, and citation export belong to `literature-evidence`.
2. **Formal review**: protocol, screening, extraction, evidence map, bias checks, and synthesis belong to `systematic-evidence-review`.
3. **Research continuity**: router, checkpoint, current stage, unresolved issues, and next action belong to `workflow-orchestration` and `project_state.yaml`.
4. **Data and statistics audit**: version, unit of analysis, repeated-measures level, methods, sample size, effect estimate, and cross-table number checks belong to `data-analysis`, `validation-and-robustness`, and `reproducibility-audit`.
5. **Reading, writing, review, figures, slides, and tone**: already owned by the academic, visualization, presentation, scoring, and humanization modules.

Therefore v4.2 strengthens evidence provenance and retrieval depth without increasing the 29 canonical modules. A recommendation count, GitHub star count, or public-account popularity is never used as a quality score by itself.
